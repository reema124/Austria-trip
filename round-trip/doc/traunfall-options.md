# Traunfall - Should We Add It?

Traunfall is a striking waterfall on the Traun river near Roitham/Steyrermühl, in the Gmunden district of Upper Austria. It's one of the most beautiful waterfalls in Austria — 12 meters high and ~200 meters long, stretching diagonally across the riverbed.

---

## What's There?

- **Traunfall waterfall** - 12m drop, ~200m long diagonal waterfall across the Traun river. Unique shape — extends sideways rather than straight down.
- **Footbridge** - New footbridge (installed 2018) directly over the Traun at the falls, with perfect viewing spot.
- **Free entry + free parking** - Gravel car park, ~5 min walk to the falls.
- **Easy access** - Flat paths, no climbing. Good for everyone.

**Time needed:** 15-20 min total (5 min walk from car + 10-15 min at the falls). It's a quick stop, not a long attraction.

---

## Where It Fits: Day 8 (Werfen → Gmunden → Melk → Vienna)

Traunfall is near Roitham, which sits **between Gmunden and the A1 motorway** heading east to Melk. It's a natural detour on the Gmunden → Melk segment.

## Option A: Add Traunfall as a Quick Stop Between Gmunden and Melk

```
Werfen → Gmunden/Traunsee (1h 45min)
    STOP: Schloss Ort castle on lake (30-45 min)
Gmunden → Traunfall/Roitham (~15 min)
    STOP: Traunfall waterfall (15-20 min)
Traunfall → Melk (~1h 10min)
    STOP: Melk Abbey (1.5-2h)
Melk → Dürnstein (30min)
    STOP: Wine, church, lunch (1-1.5h)
Dürnstein → Vienna (1h 20min)

Total driving: ~4h 55min (+5 min vs current)
Total stops: 4 stops (~4-4.5h)
Arrive Vienna: ~18:30
```

### Pros
- **Barely adds any time** — Traunfall is almost on the way between Gmunden and Melk (adds ~5 min driving + 15-20 min stop)
- Free entry, free parking, no tickets needed
- Quick and easy — not a big commitment, just a scenic 15-minute detour
- Breaks the Gmunden → Melk segment (1h 15min) into two shorter drives (15min + 1h 10min)
- Beautiful photo opportunity — the diagonal waterfall + footbridge is unique
- Open year-round, no seasonal closures

### Cons
- **4 stops on Day 8** (Gmunden + Traunfall + Melk + Dürnstein) — could feel like a lot
- Arrive Vienna ~30 min later (~18:30 instead of ~18:00)
- It's a quick nature stop, not a major attraction — some may feel it's not worth the extra stop
- Day 8 is already the busiest driving day

---

## Option B: Combine Traunfall with the Gmunden Stop

Instead of a separate stop, visit Traunfall first (it's before Gmunden coming from Werfen), then continue to Gmunden.

```
Werfen → Traunfall/Roitham (~1h 30min)
    STOP: Traunfall waterfall (15-20 min)
Traunfall → Gmunden (~15 min)
    STOP: Schloss Ort castle, quick lakeside look (20-30 min)
Gmunden → Melk (1h 15min)
    STOP: Melk Abbey (1.5-2h)
Melk → Dürnstein (30min)
    STOP: Wine, church, lunch (1-1.5h)
Dürnstein → Vienna (1h 20min)

Total driving: ~4h 50min (same as current!)
Total stops: 4 stops, but Traunfall is just a quick break (~4-4.5h)
Arrive Vienna: ~18:20
```

### Pros
- **Same total driving time** — Traunfall is on the way, not a detour
- Breaks up the longest segment (1h 45min Werfen → Gmunden) into 1h 30min + 15min
- All the same pros as Option A (free, easy, scenic, quick)
- Traunfall serves as a natural "stretch your legs" stop after the longest drive

### Cons
- Gmunden stop may need to be slightly shorter (20-30 min instead of 30-45 min)
- Same "4 stops" feeling, though Traunfall is more of a quick leg-stretch than a full stop
- Arrive Vienna ~20 min later

---

## Recommendation

**Option B (combine with Gmunden segment)** is the better choice because:

1. Traunfall is literally on the route from Werfen to Gmunden — zero detour
2. It breaks the longest Day 8 segment (1h 45min) into two comfortable pieces
3. It's only 15-20 min — more of a rest stop with a view than a full attraction
4. Same total driving time as the current plan
5. Free, easy, open year-round — no planning needed

Think of it as a "scenic rest stop" rather than a fourth major attraction. You pull over, walk 5 minutes, see an impressive waterfall, take photos from the footbridge, and continue to Gmunden refreshed.
