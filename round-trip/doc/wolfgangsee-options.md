# Wolfgangsee - Should We Add It?

St. Wolfgang am Wolfgangsee is one of Austria's most charming lake towns, sitting in the heart of the Salzkammergut. It fits into **Day 8 (Apr 9)** on the drive from Werfen back to Vienna.

---

## What's There?

- **Weisses Rössl (White Horse Inn)** - The legendary lakeside hotel from the famous operetta. Beautiful terrace right on the water.
- **Schafbergbahn** - Historic cog railway (since 1893) climbing Schafberg mountain (1,783m). Panoramic views over 7 lakes from the summit. **Check if running in early April** - usually opens late April/May.
- **Pilgrimage Church of St. Wolfgang** - Stunning church with the famous Michael Pacher altarpiece (1481), one of the finest Gothic altarpieces in the world.
- **Lakeside promenade** - Turquoise water, mountain backdrop, cafes along the shore.
- **Boat rides** - Ferry across the lake (if running in April).

**Time needed:** 45 min for a quick walk + church, 1.5-2h if you do the Schafbergbahn (likely closed in April though).

## Option 1: Add Wolfgangsee + Keep Gmunden (4 stops)

```
Werfen → St. Wolfgang (1h)
    STOP: Wolfgangsee village + church (45 min)
St. Wolfgang → Gmunden (35 min)
    STOP: Schloss Ort, quick lakeside look (20-30 min)
Gmunden → Melk (1h 15min)
    STOP: Melk Abbey (1.5-2h)
Melk → Dürnstein (30min)
    STOP: Wine, church, lunch (1-1.5h)
Dürnstein → Vienna (1h 20min)

Total driving: ~4h 40min
Total stops: 4 stops (~4-4.5h)
Arrive Vienna: ~19:00
```

### Pros
- See both Wolfgangsee AND Traunsee/Schloss Ort - two of Salzkammergut's best lakes
- Breaks up the longest segment (1h 45min becomes 1h + 35min)
- All driving segments well under 2 hours
- Wolfgangsee is arguably the prettiest stop on the whole trip

### Cons
- **4 stops in one day** - could feel rushed, more "drive-stop-drive" than relaxed
- Arrive in Vienna ~1 hour later (~19:00 instead of ~18:00)
- Less time for Vienna evening (Stephansdom, Naschmarkt closes at 18:00, dinner)
- Gmunden stop would need to be shorter (20-30 min instead of 45 min)
- Long day overall: depart 8:00, arrive 19:00 = 11 hours on the road

---

## Option 2: Replace Gmunden with Wolfgangsee (3 stops)

```
Werfen → St. Wolfgang (1h)
    STOP: Wolfgangsee village + church (45 min - 1h)
St. Wolfgang → Melk (1h 45min)
    STOP: Melk Abbey (1.5-2h)
Melk → Dürnstein (30min)
    STOP: Wine, church, lunch (1-1.5h)
Dürnstein → Vienna (1h 20min)

Total driving: ~4h 35min
Total stops: 3 stops (~3.5-4h)
Arrive Vienna: ~18:00
```

### Pros
- **Same number of stops (3)** as the original plan - not rushed
- Wolfgangsee is more charming and famous than Gmunden
- Same arrival time in Vienna (~18:00) - full evening available
- All segments under 2 hours
- Still get the "Salzkammergut lake experience" but with a more iconic town
- The Pilgrimage Church alone is worth the stop

### Cons
- **Miss Schloss Ort / Traunsee** - the castle-on-the-lake is a unique photo spot
- **St. Wolfgang → Melk is 1h 45min** - right at the 2-hour comfort limit (vs 1h 15min from Gmunden)
- Gmunden is slightly more "on the way" geographically (Wolfgangsee is a small detour south)

---

## Option 3: Add Wolfgangsee on Day 4 (Hallstatt → Zell am See)

Instead of Day 8, add Wolfgangsee as a stop on Day 4 when driving from Hallstatt to Zell am See.

```
Hallstatt → St. Wolfgang (35 min)
    STOP: Wolfgangsee village + church (45 min - 1h)
St. Wolfgang → Zell am See (1h 20min)

Total driving: ~1h 55min (vs 1h 45min direct)
Total stops: 1 stop (~45 min - 1h)
```

### Current Day 4 (without Wolfgangsee)

```
Hallstatt → Zell am See (1h 45min, no stops)
    Arrive ~11:45, full afternoon in Zell am See
```

### Updated Day 4 (with Wolfgangsee)

```
09:00  Depart Hallstatt
09:35  Arrive St. Wolfgang / Wolfgangsee
         → Pilgrimage Church (Pacher altarpiece)
         → Lakeside walk, Weisses Rössl terrace
         → Coffee/snack
10:30  Depart St. Wolfgang
11:50  Arrive Zell am See
         → Afternoon as planned (lake promenade, Old Town, Schmittenhöhe)
```

### Pros
- **Day 4 currently has NO stops** — it's just a 1h 45min drive. Adding Wolfgangsee makes the day richer
- **Doesn't affect Day 8 at all** — keep Gmunden, Melk, Dürnstein, Vienna evening as planned
- Geographically natural — St. Wolfgang is between Hallstatt and Zell am See (small detour south)
- Only adds ~10 min of driving (1h 55min total vs 1h 45min direct)
- Arrive in Zell am See at ~11:50 instead of ~11:45 — practically no difference
- Breaks the emptiest day of driving into two scenic segments
- Both driving segments well under 2 hours (35min + 1h 20min)

### Cons
- **Easter Saturday (Apr 5)** — the Pilgrimage Church and village should still be open, but some cafes may have limited hours
- Slightly less time in Zell am See afternoon (arrive ~12:00 instead of ~11:45, so only ~15 min difference)
- The detour through St. Wolfgang adds mountain roads that may be slower in April
- If Gosausee morning detour is also done, the morning gets busier

---

## Recommendation

**Option 3 (Day 4)** is actually the best choice for this trip because:

1. **Day 4 is currently the emptiest driving day** — just a 1h 45min drive with no stops. Adding Wolfgangsee fills that gap perfectly
2. **Doesn't sacrifice anything on Day 8** — you keep Gmunden/Schloss Ort, Melk Abbey, Dürnstein, AND arrive in Vienna at 18:00
3. **Almost zero time cost** — only adds ~10 min of driving and you still arrive in Zell am See by noon
4. St. Wolfgang / Wolfgangsee is consistently rated as one of Austria's top 5 most beautiful places
5. The Pilgrimage Church with the Pacher altarpiece is genuinely world-class art
6. Two short driving segments (35min + 1h 20min) instead of one medium one (1h 45min)

The only downside is Easter Saturday, but the church and village should be accessible.

**If Option 3 doesn't work** (e.g., Easter closures), then **Option 2** is the next best — replace Gmunden with Wolfgangsee on Day 8 to keep 3 stops and the same Vienna arrival time.
