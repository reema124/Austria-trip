### IMPORTANT: Easter 2026 = April 5 (Day 4!)

Easter Sunday falls right in the middle of your trip. This means:
- **Good Friday (Apr 3) through Easter Monday (Apr 6)** is a major holiday period.
- Some restaurants/shops may have **limited hours or be closed**, especially on Easter Sunday.
- Domestic tourism increases - **book accommodation early** as prices rise for Easter week.
- **Hallstatt Salt Mine** may have special Easter hours - verify before visiting.
- **Salzburg Easter Festival** (classical music/opera) may coincide - check salzburger-osterfestspiele.at if interested.

### Before You Go - Essentials

- **Austria Vignette**: Required for all Austrian motorways. Buy a 10-day digital vignette (~EUR 9.90) at asfinag.at. **CRITICAL: The digital vignette takes effect 18 days after purchase. Buy by March 14 at the latest!** Alternatively, buy a physical sticker at a gas station (OMV, BP, Shell) near the border on arrival.
- **Slovakia Vignette**: Slovakia also requires a highway e-vignette. Buy at eznamka.sk before entering Slovak motorways (~EUR 12 for 10 days).
- **Winter tires**: Legally required in Austria through April 15. If renting a car in Austria, it will already have winter tires. Confirm with your rental agency.
- **Fuel**: Fill up in Slovakia where fuel is cheaper (~EUR 1.40-1.60/liter in Austria).
- **Currency**: Euro (both Slovakia and Austria use EUR).
- **Weather in early April**: Unpredictable. Valleys ~5-15C, mountain areas ~0-8C. Pack layers, rain jacket, and comfortable walking shoes. Snow possible at higher elevations.
- **Snow chains**: Recommended to have in the car for mountain passes (Zell am See/Hallstatt area), though main roads are usually clear by April. Rentals often offer them as add-on (~EUR 20-30).
- **Parking**: Most Austrian cities have paid parking zones (Kurzparkzonen). Keep coins ready or use the EasyPark / ParkNow app.
- **Language**: German in Austria, Slovak in Bratislava. English widely understood in tourist areas.

### Things to Verify 2-4 Weeks Before Travel

1. Dachstein Krippenstein cable car / Five Fingers - April operating status (dachstein-salzkammergut.com)
2. Hallstatt Salt Mine + Skywalk - 2026 opening date (salzwelten.at)
3. Kitzsteinhorn glacier cable car - April hours (kitzsteinhorn.at)
4. Schmittenhohe cable car - April operation (schmittenhohe.at)
5. Sigmund-Thun Gorge - likely not open in early April

7. St. Wolfgang Pilgrimage Church - Easter Saturday opening hours
8. Schafbergbahn cog railway - unlikely to be running in April (usually opens late April/May)
9. Easter closures for restaurants/shops (Apr 3-6)

### DAY 1 - Tuesday night April 1 + Wednesday April 2 | Bratislava

**Arrival:** Flight lands 1:30 AM on April 1
**Check-in:** April 1 (late night) | **Check-out:** April 3
**Drive:** None
**Sleep:** Park Inn by Radisson Danube, Bratislava (2 nights)

#### April 1 - Arrival Night

- Land at 1:30 AM. Taxi/transfer to Park Inn by Radisson Danube (~20 min from BTS airport).
- Sleep! Full day starts tomorrow.

#### April 2 - Full Day in Bratislava

#### Morning / Afternoon - Old Town

- **Hlavne namestie (Main Square)** - Heart of the old town, beautiful pastel-colored buildings. Start your walk here.
- **Michael's Gate (Michalska brana)** - The only preserved gate of the medieval fortifications. Climb the tower for city views (~EUR 5).
- **Old Town Hall** - Explore the courtyard and city museum inside.
- **Cumil (Man at Work statue)** - The famous bronze man peeking out of a manhole. Great photo spot on Panenska/Laurinska street corner.
- **St. Martin's Cathedral** - Gothic cathedral where Hungarian kings were crowned. Free entry.
- **UFO Bridge observation deck** - Take the elevator up for panoramic views of Bratislava and the Danube (~EUR 8). Worth it at sunset.

#### Late Afternoon - Bratislava Castle

- **Bratislava Castle (Bratislavsky hrad)** - Walk up from Old Town (~15 min uphill). The castle terrace has stunning views over the Danube and into Austria. The Slovak National Museum inside is optional. Free to walk the grounds, museum ~EUR 10.
- Sunset from the castle gardens is magical.

#### Evening - Little Carpathians Wine

- Bratislava sits at the edge of the **Little Carpathians wine region**, known for excellent white wines (Gruner Veltliner, Riesling, Devin).
- **Wine bars in Old Town**: Try local Slovak wines at a "vinoteka."
- Recommended spots:
  - **Vinoteka sv. Urbana** - Great selection of Slovak wines, cozy cellar atmosphere.
  - **Zylinder Cafe & Restaurant** - Good Slovak cuisine in a stylish setting on Hviezdoslavovo namestie.
  - **Slovak Pub** - Huge traditional restaurant in a labyrinth of themed rooms. Fun atmosphere, big portions, very affordable.

#### Sleeping - Bratislava

- **Park Inn by Radisson Danube** - Already booked. Right on the Danube waterfront.
- **Expect to pay**: EUR 50-90/night for a double room mid-range.

#### Meals & Food Tips

- **Breakfast**: Most hotels include breakfast. Otherwise, try **Mondieu** on Panenska for excellent brunch.
- **Lunch**: Grab a quick bite at **Street Food Park** or sit down at **Flagship Restaurant** on the Danube waterfront.
- **Dinner**: Slovak Pub for traditional halushky (bryndzove halusky - potato dumplings with sheep cheese and bacon). It's a must-try national dish.
- **Budget tip**: Bratislava is significantly cheaper than Austria. Enjoy bigger meals here!

### DAY 2 - Thursday, April 3 | Bratislava --> Hallstatt (Drive Day)

**Drive:** Bratislava --> Semmering (~1h 30min) --> Bad Aussee (~2h) --> Hallstatt (~20min)
**Total driving: ~4 hours (broken into 3 segments, 2 stops)**
**Sleep:** Hallstatt / Obertraun

#### Morning - Check Out + Drive Segment 1 (8:00 - 10:00)

- Check out of Park Inn, depart Bratislava ~8:00 AM.
- **Buy your Austria Vignette** at a gas station right after crossing the border if you haven't already.
- Drive south on the A2 motorway toward Wiener Neustadt, then continue to Semmering.

**Drive: Bratislava --> Semmering Pass (~1h 30min)**

#### Stop 1 - Semmering Pass (10:00 - 10:30)

- **Semmering Railway** - A UNESCO World Heritage Site. The world's first mountain railway, built 1848-1854, with stunning viaducts, tunnels, and mountain scenery.
- Quick stop for **coffee and photos** at Semmering village. The viaducts are visible from the road and the village viewpoints.
- Not a long stop - just enough to stretch legs and appreciate the engineering marvel.

#### Drive Segment 2 - Semmering --> Bad Aussee (~2h, 10:30 - 12:30)

- Continue through the Mur Valley via the S6 and A9 motorways.
- The landscape becomes progressively more Alpine as you head toward the Salzkammergut.

#### Stop 2 - Bad Aussee / Ausseerland (12:30 - 13:30)

- **Bad Aussee** - A charming small spa town, gateway to the Ausseerland. It sits at the confluence of two rivers surrounded by mountains.
- **Lunch stop**: Grab a meal at a local Gasthaus. Try **Gasthof Blaue Traube** on the main square, or **Lebzelterhaus** for traditional cakes and coffee.
- Quick walk through the pretty town center (~20 min).
- **Alternative**: If time allows, detour to **Altaussee** (10 min further) - a stunning mountain lake with the Loser mountain backdrop. There's also a historic salt mine here.

#### Drive Segment 3 - Bad Aussee --> Hallstatt (~20min, 13:30 - 14:00)

- Short, scenic drive along the road to Hallstatt.

#### Afternoon - Arrive in Hallstatt (14:00 - 18:00)

**Hallstatt** is arguably Austria's most photographed village. Tiny, lakeside, with mountains rising dramatically on all sides.

**Arrival tips:**
- **Parking**: Hallstatt has extremely limited parking. Use the **P1 parking garage** at the tunnel entrance (north end of village). ~EUR 10/day. Do NOT try to drive into the village.
- Walk from the parking garage into the village (~5 min).

**Afternoon highlights:**
- **The Classic Viewpoint** - Walk north along the lake path for THE iconic photo of Hallstatt with the church and lake. It's on the road between the parking and the village.
- **Marktplatz (Market Square)** - The heart of the village. Tiny, picturesque, surrounded by flower-covered wooden houses.
- **Hallstatt Catholic Church** - Pretty lakeside church.
- **Beinhaus (Bone House)** - In the Catholic churchyard. A small ossuary with over 600 painted skulls - a unique and slightly eerie tradition since the 12th century. ~EUR 2.
- **Lake Walk** - Walk south along the waterfront for peaceful views and fewer crowds.

#### Evening

- Hallstatt gets incredibly quiet after day-trippers leave (~17:00). This is the magic time - the village practically empties and you have it almost to yourself.
- Walk the waterfront at sunset. The reflections on the lake are extraordinary.
- **Dinner**: **Gasthof Zauner** - Traditional lakeside restaurant, excellent fresh fish from the lake (trout!). Or **Seehotel Gruner Baum** restaurant for lakefront terrace dining.

#### Sleeping - Hallstatt area (2 nights: Apr 3-4)

- **In Hallstatt**: Accommodation is limited and pricey. Book well in advance.
  - **Heritage Hotel Hallstatt** - Right on the lake, beautiful. Premium price.
  - **Gasthof Zauner** - Has rooms above the restaurant. Central, historic.
  - **Braugasthof Hallstatt** - Slightly outside center, more affordable, has parking.
- **In Obertraun** (5 min drive, much cheaper, closer to Dachstein):
  - **Haus Loidl** - Budget guesthouse.
  - **B&B Hallstatt** - Good value with mountain views.
- **Area tip**: Obertraun is better value and gives you easy access to tomorrow's Dachstein activities.
- **Expect to pay**: EUR 100-180/night in Hallstatt, EUR 60-100 in Obertraun.

### DAY 3 - Friday, April 4 | Hallstatt + Dachstein

**Drive:** Short local drives (~15-30 min each)
**Sleep:** Hallstatt area

#### Morning - Hallstatt Salt Mine + Skywalk (9:00 - 12:00)

- **Hallstatt Salt Mine (Salzwelten)** - The world's oldest salt mine. Take the funicular up and then a mining railway inside. Includes an underground salt lake and the oldest wooden staircase in Europe.
  - ~EUR 40 (includes funicular + mine tour).
  - Duration: ~2.5 hours total.
  - **Hallstatt Skywalk** - Included with the salt mine ticket. A viewing platform 350m above the lake with spectacular views.
  - **Check April opening** - Usually opens late March/early April. Verify dates at salzwelten.at.
- **Hallstatt Museum** - If salt mine is closed, visit this instead. Covers 7,000 years of salt mining history. ~EUR 10.

#### Afternoon - Dachstein Krippenstein (12:30 - 17:00)

- Drive to **Obertraun** cable car station (~5-15 min from Hallstatt).

**Important April note**: Operations depend on weather and season. Check dachstein-salzkammergut.com before going.

**If operating:**
- **Five Fingers Viewpoint** - Five metal platforms jutting out over a 400m cliff. Each "finger" has a different design element (glass floor, picture frame, telescope). One of Austria's most spectacular viewpoints. FREE once you've bought the cable car ticket.
  - ~15 min walk from the Krippenstein summit station.
  - **Cable car cost**: ~EUR 40 round trip.

- **Dachstein Ice Cave (Eishohle)** - A network of ice formations inside the mountain. Guided tours available. ~EUR 30 (can be combined with cable car).
- **Mammoth Cave (Mammuthohle)** - Another cave system, remarkable for its sheer size.
- Duration for cable car + Five Fingers: 2-3 hours. Add 1-1.5 hours per cave.

**Alternative (if Dachstein closed): Gosausee**
- Drive to **Gosau** (~30 min from Obertraun).
- **Vorderer Gosausee** - One of Austria's most beautiful alpine lakes. The reflection of the Dachstein glacier in the emerald green water is legendary.
- **Walk around the lake** - Easy, flat trail. ~1.5 hours for the full loop.
- **Parking**: ~EUR 5.

#### Evening

- Return to Hallstatt/Obertraun.
- Last evening at Hallstatt. Enjoy a farewell Alpine dinner.
- **Dinner**: **Gasthof Koppen** in Obertraun for hearty, no-frills Austrian mountain food. Or **Braugasthof Hallstatt** for local brewery food.
- Pack and prepare for the drive to Zell am See tomorrow.

### DAY 4 - Saturday, April 5 | Hallstatt --> Zell am See

**Drive:** Hallstatt --> St. Wolfgang (~35min) --> Zell am See (~1h 20min)
**Sleep:** Zell am See

#### Morning - Depart Hallstatt (9:00 - 9:30)

- Pack up, grab breakfast.
- If you haven't done Gosausee yet, consider a quick morning detour (adds ~1h).

#### Drive: Hallstatt --> St. Wolfgang (~35min, 9:30 - 10:05)

- Short scenic drive through Salzkammergut to the shores of Wolfgangsee.

#### Stop: St. Wolfgang am Wolfgangsee (10:05 - 10:50)

- **Pilgrimage Church of St. Wolfgang** - Stunning church with the famous **Michael Pacher altarpiece (1481)**, one of the finest Gothic altarpieces in the world. Free entry.
- **Lakeside promenade** - Turquoise water, mountain backdrop. Walk past the legendary **Weisses Rossl (White Horse Inn)** from the famous operetta.
- **Quick coffee/snack** at one of the lakeside cafes.
- *Note: The Schafbergbahn cog railway (1,783m summit) likely won't be running yet in early April — usually opens late April/May.*

#### Drive: St. Wolfgang --> Zell am See (~1h 20min, 10:50 - 12:10)

- Scenic drive through Alpine passes and the Salzburg lake district.

#### Afternoon - Zell am See (12:00 - 18:00)

- **Lake Zell (Zeller See)** - Crystal-clear Alpine lake surrounded by mountains. The backdrop of the Kitzsteinhorn glacier is spectacular.
- **Esplanade / Lake Promenade** - Walk the full lakefront promenade (~45 min loop). Benches, cafes, and mountain views everywhere.
- **Old Town of Zell am See** - Small but charming. St. Hippolyte's Church, Vogtturm (city tower/museum).
- **Schmittenhohe Cable Car** - If weather is clear, take the cable car up Schmittenhohe (1,965m) for 360-degree panoramic views of 30+ peaks including the Grossglockner and Kitzsteinhorn. ~EUR 35 round trip. Check if operating in early April (may be between seasons).
- **Elisabeth Promenade** - Peaceful walk along the lake shore, especially pretty in afternoon light.

#### IMPORTANT: Easter Saturday

- **April 5 is Easter Saturday (Holy Saturday)**. Some shops may close early or be closed. Supermarkets typically have shorter hours. Restaurants are usually open but may be busier.

#### Evening

- **Dinner**: **Steinerwirt 1493** - One of the oldest inns in the region, great Austrian food. Or **Zum Hirschen** for classic Pinzgauer cuisine.
- **Relaxed evening**: Zell am See has a wonderful chill mountain-town vibe. Walk the promenade at sunset, grab a drink lakeside.

#### Sleeping - Zell am See (2 nights: Apr 5-6)

- **Budget**: Jugendherberge Zell am See (Youth Hostel), Pension Hubertus
- **Mid-range**: Hotel Neue Post (center, lakefront area), Alpin Life Hotel Gebhard
- **Area to book**: Town center near the lake - everything is compact and walkable.
- **Expect to pay**: EUR 80-130/night for a double room mid-range.

### DAY 5 - Sunday, April 6 | Zell am See Region / Kaprun

**Drive:** Short drives in the area (~10-20 min each way)
**Sleep:** Zell am See

#### IMPORTANT: Easter Sunday

- **April 6 is Easter Sunday**. Many shops and some restaurants WILL be closed. Plan accordingly - have snacks ready, know which restaurants are open. Hotel breakfast is your best friend today.

#### Morning - Kaprun & Kitzsteinhorn (9:00 - 13:00)

- Drive to **Kaprun** (~10 min from Zell am See).
- **Kitzsteinhorn (3,029m)** - Austria's only glacier ski area in this region. Even in April, you can take the cable car up to the summit.
  - **Top of Salzburg** viewing platform at 3,029m - jaw-dropping 360-degree panorama.
  - **National Park Gallery** - Panoramic tunnel carved into the mountain at 2,450m.
  - **ICE ARENA** - Walk through a gallery carved into glacial ice.
  - **Cost**: ~EUR 45 round trip cable car.
  - **Duration**: 2-3 hours up top.
  - **Important**: Check operating status for early April. Dress warmly - it will be well below 0C at the summit!

#### Afternoon - Valley Exploration (13:00 - 18:00)

**Lunch in Kaprun**
- **Dorfstadl Kaprun** - Cozy mountain restaurant, great kaiserschmarrn (shredded pancake, a must-try Austrian dessert/dish).
- **Burg Kaprun** - Medieval castle ruin, free to walk around. Nice views of the valley.

**Sigmund-Thun Klamm (Gorge)**
- A spectacular narrow gorge near Kaprun with wooden walkways over turquoise water.
- **Check if open in early April** - usually opens mid/late May. If closed, skip.
- Duration: ~45 min. ~EUR 6.

**Alternative if gorge is closed:**
- **Maiskogel** - Family-friendly mountain with a gondola. Easy walks at the top with views down to Zell am See.
- Drive the road toward **Stauseen Kaprun** (reservoir dams) - impressive high-alpine engineering and scenery. Road may be closed in early April due to snow - check conditions.

#### Evening

- Return to Zell am See.
- **Dinner**: **Seewirt** - Lake-view dining. Or try **Deins & Meins** for a more modern bistro feel.
- Take an evening stroll along the lake. April sunsets over the mountains are incredible.

### DAY 6 - Monday, April 7 | Zell am See --> Werfen + Salzburg Evening

**Drive:** Zell am See --> Werfen (~50 min) + Werfen --> Salzburg (~40 min evening round trip)
**Sleep:** Werfen (Der Burgblick - Adults Only)

#### Morning - Last Lake Walk (9:00 - 10:00)

- Leisurely breakfast at the hotel.
- Final walk along Lake Zell if the weather is nice.
- Pack up and check out.

#### Drive: Zell am See --> Werfen (~50 min, 10:00 - 10:50)

- Short drive north on the B311. Scenic Salzach Valley with mountains on both sides.
- Check in at **Der Burgblick - Adults Only** in Werfen.

#### Afternoon - Hohenwerfen Castle (11:00 - 13:30)

- **Hohenwerfen Castle (Burg Hohenwerfen)** - A dramatic 900-year-old fortress perched on a cliff 155m above the Salzach Valley. Right in Werfen!
- **Falconry show** - The castle hosts daily birds-of-prey demonstrations (eagles, falcons, vultures). One of Austria's best-kept secrets.
- **Cost**: ~EUR 16 adults (includes elevator + fortress + falconry show).
- **Duration**: 1.5-2 hours.
- **Tip**: The castle was a filming location for "Where Eagles Dare" (1968).

#### Lunch in Werfen

- **Obauer** - If you want to splurge, this is a 2-Michelin-star restaurant right in Werfen village. One of Austria's best restaurants.
- **Gasthof Lebzelter** - More casual, traditional Austrian fare. Good value.

#### Late Afternoon - Drive to Salzburg (16:30 - 17:10)

**Drive: Werfen --> Salzburg (~40 min)**

#### Evening - Salzburg Old Town Walk (17:00 - 21:00)

- Walk through the **Altstadt (Old Town)** - UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- **Getreidegasse** - Salzburg's most famous shopping street with ornate wrought-iron guild signs. Mozart's birthplace is here (visit tomorrow).
- **Residenzplatz** - Grand square with a large fountain. Atmospheric in the evening.
- **Salzach River** - Walk along the river embankment as the fortress lights up above.

#### Dinner in Salzburg

- **Stiftskeller St. Peter** - One of Europe's oldest restaurants (803 AD!). Traditional Austrian food in a stunning setting. Book ahead.
- **Zwettler's** - Hearty Austrian classics (Schnitzel, Tafelspitz) right on Kaigasse.
- **Budget**: Grab a Bosna (spicy sausage in a bun, Salzburg's street food specialty) from a stand on Getreidegasse.

#### Drive back to Werfen (~40 min, ~21:00)

- Return to Der Burgblick for the night.

### DAY 7 - Tuesday, April 8 | Salzburg Full Day (from Werfen)

**Drive:** Werfen --> Salzburg (~40 min each way)
**Sleep:** Werfen (Der Burgblick)

#### Morning - Drive to Salzburg + Hohensalzburg Fortress (8:30 - 12:00)

**Drive: Werfen --> Salzburg (~40 min)**

- **Hohensalzburg Fortress** - Salzburg's crown jewel. One of the largest and best-preserved medieval castles in Europe.
- **Getting there**: Take the **funicular** from Festungsgasse (included in ticket) or walk up (~20 min).
- **Cost**: ~EUR 16.30 (includes funicular + all rooms).
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours to explore fully.
- **Highlights**: State rooms, torture chamber, Rainer Regiment museum, and the **panoramic terrace** with views over the entire city, river, and Alps.
- **Tip**: Go early to avoid crowds.

**Salzburg Cathedral (Dom)**
- Right at the foot of the fortress hill. Magnificent Baroque cathedral.
- **Free entry** (donation appreciated). The baptismal font where Mozart was baptized is inside.
- Duration: 30 min.

#### Lunch (12:00 - 13:00)

- **Triangel** - Locals' favorite near Wiener-Philharmoniker-Gasse. Great Schnitzel.
- **Cafe Tomaselli** - Salzburg's oldest coffeehouse (since 1705) on Alter Markt. Perfect for coffee and cake (try Salzburger Nockerl - a sweet souffle, the city's signature dessert).

#### Afternoon - Mirabell + City Exploration (13:00 - 17:00)

**Mirabell Palace & Gardens (13:00 - 14:00)**
- Beautiful Baroque gardens, famous from "The Sound of Music" (Do-Re-Mi scene).
- **Free entry** to the gardens.
- The Pegasus fountain, rose garden, and dwarf garden are the highlights.
- The Marble Hall inside the palace is one of the most beautiful wedding halls in the world (free to peek in).

**Afternoon exploration (14:00 - 17:00)**
- **Mozart's Birthplace (Getreidegasse 9)** - Worth a visit for music lovers (~EUR 14). See his childhood violin, family portraits, and manuscripts.
- **Kapuzinerberg Hill** - Cross the river and hike up for a quieter, less-touristy viewpoint over Salzburg. ~15 min hike.
- **Linzergasse** - The Neustadt's charming shopping street, less crowded than Getreidegasse.

#### Evening - Cafe + Beer Hall (17:00 - 20:30)

- **Cafe Sacher Salzburg** - Have a slice of the original Sacher Torte on their terrace overlooking the river. Touristy but worth the experience.
- Walk along the Salzach at golden hour. Cross the **Makartsteg bridge** (love-lock bridge) for photos.
- **Dinner**: **Gasthof Goldgasse** - Refined Austrian cuisine in a medieval building. Or **Blaue Gans** for a more modern take on Austrian food.
- **Beer option**: **Augustiner Brau** - A massive beer hall/garden run by monks. Self-service, local favorite, beer tapped from wooden barrels. Amazing atmosphere.

#### Drive back to Werfen (~40 min, ~20:30)

- Return to Der Burgblick. Last night in Werfen - enjoy the castle views one more time.

### DAY 8 - Wednesday, April 9 | Werfen --> Wachau --> Vienna (Drive Day)

**Drive:** Werfen --> Traunfall (~1h 30min) --> Gmunden (~15min) --> Melk (~1h 15min) --> Durnstein (~30min) --> Vienna (~1h 20min)
**Total driving: ~4h 50min (broken into 5 segments, 4 stops)**
**Sleep:** Vienna

#### Morning - Depart Werfen (8:00)

- Early start for the big driving day. Check out of Der Burgblick.
- Head north on the A10, then east on the A1 motorway.

#### Drive Segment 1: Werfen --> Traunfall/Roitham (~1h 30min, 8:00 - 9:30)

- Drive through the scenic Salzburg lake district. You'll pass near Mondsee and the Attersee.
- Take the Laakirchen West exit off the A1, follow B144 toward Roitham.

#### Stop 1 - Traunfall (9:30 - 9:50)

- **Traunfall** - One of Austria's most beautiful waterfalls. 12 meters high and ~200 meters long, stretching diagonally across the Traun river — a unique shape you won't see elsewhere.
- **Footbridge** - Walk across the new footbridge (2018) directly over the falls for perfect views and photos.
- **Free entry, free parking**. Gravel car park, ~5 min walk to the falls.
- Duration: 15-20 min — a scenic rest stop to stretch your legs after the longest drive segment.

#### Drive Segment 2: Traunfall --> Gmunden (~15min, 9:50 - 10:05)

- Short drive north to the shores of Traunsee.

#### Stop 2 - Gmunden / Traunsee (10:05 - 10:30)

- **Gmunden** sits on the northern shore of **Traunsee**, one of Austria's deepest and most beautiful lakes.
- **Schloss Ort (Seeschloss Ort)** - A fairy-tale castle on a small island in the lake, connected by a wooden bridge. Iconic Austrian postcard motif. Free to walk across and photograph.
- **Esplanade walk** - Quick stroll along the lakefront with mountain backdrop.
- Duration: 20-30 min stop.

#### Drive Segment 3: Gmunden --> Melk (~1h 15min, 10:30 - 11:45)

- Continue east on the A1/A9 through upper Austria toward the Danube.

#### Stop 3 - Melk Abbey (11:45 - 13:30)

- **Melk Abbey (Stift Melk)** - One of Europe's most magnificent Baroque monasteries, perched on a cliff above the Danube.
- **Hours**: Opens 9:00 AM (April schedule).
- **Duration**: 1.5-2 hours for the full tour.
- **Cost**: ~EUR 14.50 adults with guided tour.
- **Highlights**: Imperial corridor, marble hall, incredible library (250,000+ volumes), terrace overlooking the Danube Valley.
- **Parking**: Large lot at the base of the abbey (~EUR 4).
- **Tip**: This is the gateway to the Wachau Valley - one of Austria's most scenic stretches.

#### Drive Segment 4: Melk --> Durnstein (~30min, 13:30 - 14:00)

- Drive east along the Danube through the **Wachau Valley**. This is one of Austria's most scenic drives - vineyards, apricot orchards, and the blue Danube winding below.
- **Apricot blossom season!** Early April is peak bloom for Wachau's famous Marillen (apricot) orchards. The valley may be covered in white/pink blossoms.

#### Stop 4 - Durnstein (14:00 - 15:30)

- **Durnstein** is a tiny, picture-perfect village famous for:
  - **Blue church tower** - The iconic Baroque Stiftskirche, visible from far away.
  - **Ruine Durnstein** - Ruins of the castle where Richard the Lionheart was imprisoned in 1192. ~20 min hike up, great valley views.
  - **Wine tasting** - Wachau is one of Austria's premier wine regions. Try Gruner Veltliner at a local Heuriger (wine tavern).
- **Lunch**: **Loibnerhof** for Danube fish and local wines. Budget option: grab a Wachauer Laberl (local bread) with cheese at a bakery.
- Duration: 1-1.5 hours.

#### Drive Segment 5: Durnstein --> Vienna (~1h 20min, 15:30 - 16:50)

- Join the A1/S33 motorway eastward to Vienna.

#### Afternoon/Evening - Arrive in Vienna (~17:00)

- Check into accommodation, drop bags.
- **Parking in Vienna**: Expensive and tricky. If staying central, park at the hotel or use a Park & Ride at the city edge (e.g., Erdberg P+R) and use public transport. The Vienna Wiener Linien 24h ticket costs just ~EUR 8.

#### Evening - City Walk + Dinner (18:00 - 22:00)

- **Stephansdom (St. Stephen's Cathedral)** - Vienna's Gothic masterpiece. Free to enter the main nave. South Tower climb (~EUR 6, 343 steps) has incredible city views.
- **Graben & Kohlmarkt** - Vienna's most elegant shopping streets.
- **Naschmarkt** - Vienna's most popular open-air market. 120+ stalls. May still be open when you arrive (closes ~18:00 weekdays).

#### Dinner

- **Figlmuller** - Vienna's most famous Schnitzel restaurant (since 1905). The schnitzel hangs over the plate. Book ahead or expect a queue.
- **Alternative**: **Plachutta** for the definitive Tafelspitz (boiled beef, Vienna's signature dish).
- **After dinner walk**: The **Ringstrasse** is magical at night. Walk past the illuminated Opera House, Parliament, Rathaus (City Hall), and Burgtheater.
- **Drinks**: **Loos American Bar** - Tiny, legendary cocktail bar designed by Adolf Loos in 1908. An architectural gem.

#### Sleeping - Vienna (1 night: Apr 9)

- **Budget**: Wombat's City Hostel (Naschmarkt), SafeStay Vienna
- **Mid-range**: Hotel Motel One Wien-Staatsoper (great location near Opera), Ruby Marie Hotel
- **Area to book**: Innere Stadt (1st district) or close by along the Ring (4th, 6th, 7th districts are great value).
- **Expect to pay**: EUR 90-160/night for a double room mid-range.

### DAY 9 - Thursday, April 10 | Vienna (Final Day + Departure)

**Drive:** None / To airport
**Sleep:** --

#### Morning - Last Vienna Highlights (9:00 - 13:00)

- **Schonbrunn Palace** - The Habsburgs' summer residence. Even if you skip the interior (~EUR 22), the gardens are free and spectacular. The **Gloriette** viewpoint over the palace and city is a must.
  - Take the U4 metro to Schonbrunn station.
  - Duration: 1.5-2 hours for gardens + Gloriette.

- **Cafe Central** - Have breakfast/brunch at Vienna's most famous coffeehouse. Stunning neo-Gothic interior. Where Trotsky, Freud, and countless intellectuals used to gather. Try a Melange (Viennese coffee) and an Apfelstrudel.

- **Alternative morning**: **Belvedere Palace** - Houses Klimt's famous "The Kiss." Beautiful Baroque palace with gardens. ~EUR 16.

#### Midday - Friends Depart / Trip Ends

- Depending on departure plans (flights, driving back), wrap up the trip.
- If flying from Vienna Airport (VIE): ~20 min drive from city center or take the CAT (City Airport Train) from Wien Mitte.
