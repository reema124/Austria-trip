Before You Go

Austria Vignette

Required for all motorways. 10-day digital vignette ~EUR 9.90 at asfinag.at. CRITICAL: Digital vignette activates 18 days after purchase — buy by March 14! Or buy a physical sticker at a gas station on arrival.

Slovakia Vignette

Also required for Slovak motorways! Buy the e-vignette at eznamka.sk before entering (~EUR 12 for 10 days).

Weather (Early April)

Valleys 5-15°C, mountains 0-8°C. Pack layers, rain jacket, warm fleece for cable cars. Snow possible at altitude.

Currency & Fuel

Euro in both countries. Fill up in Slovakia where fuel is cheaper. Austria ~EUR 1.40-1.60/liter.

Winter Tires

Legally required in Austria through April 15. Rental cars should have them — confirm with agency. Snow chains recommended for mountain areas.

Parking

Most Austrian cities have paid zones (Kurzparkzonen). Keep coins ready or use EasyPark / ParkNow app. Hallstatt: use P1 garage only!

Arrival

Flight lands 1:30 AM. Taxi/transfer to Park Inn by Radisson Danube (~20 min from BTS airport). Sleep!

Morning

Old Town Walk

  • Hlavne namestie (Main Square) — Pastel-colored buildings, start here
  • Michael's Gate — Only preserved medieval gate, tower views (~EUR 5)
  • Cumil (Man at Work) — Famous bronze statue, great photo spot
  • St. Martin's Cathedral — Gothic cathedral, free entry
  • UFO Bridge deck — Panoramic Danube views (~EUR 8), great at sunset
Late Afternoon

Bratislava Castle

Walk up from Old Town (~15 min). Stunning views over the Danube and into Austria. Free grounds, museum ~EUR 10. Sunset from the castle gardens is magical.

Evening

Little Carpathians Wine + Dinner

Slovak Pub
Huge traditional restaurant in themed rooms. Try bryndzove halusky (potato dumplings with sheep cheese). Affordable!
  • Vinoteka sv. Urbana — Slovak wines, cozy cellar
  • Zylinder Cafe — Stylish Slovak cuisine on Hviezdoslavovo namestie
Budget tip: Bratislava is significantly cheaper than Austria. Enjoy bigger meals here!

Hallstatt Village

Austria's most photographed village. Tiny, lakeside, mountains rising on all sides.

Parking: Use P1 parking garage at the tunnel entrance. ~EUR 10/day. Do NOT try to drive into the village!
  • The Classic Viewpoint — THE iconic photo between parking and village
  • Marktplatz — Tiny market square, flower-covered houses
